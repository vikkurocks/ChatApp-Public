QUICK GUIDE

Make the most of ChatApp.

A private room for real-time conversations, sharing moments and watching videos together. Follow these steps to get started.

01

Create or join a room

Enter your username, room name and a password of at least 8 characters. Friends should enter the same room name and password to join your room.

02

Chat and share

Send real-time messages, reply to a message, use emojis and share images. Use a username that your friends can recognize.

03

Watch Together

Open Watch inside the room and share a supported YouTube link. Playback controls are synchronized with room members. MP4 upload availability depends on server storage configuration.

04

Voice and video calls

Start a call with a room member. Allow microphone and camera permissions when your browser asks, and keep a stable network connection.

Privacy tip: Share your room name and password only with people you want to invite. For calls, use ChatApp over HTTPS and allow camera/microphone access.
